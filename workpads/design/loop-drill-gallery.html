Lane-drawer inside the loop

Option 2 — the ×4 critique fan expands as full cards right inside the loop box, exactly like a flat fan does in a phase lane. The dashed-amber LOOP compound is the canvas. Each round's body is a real sequence: a ×4 critique fan-out → a decision diamond → a revise step. Selecting round 2 expands its four critics in place as solid cards in a 2×2 grid — each with label · status dot · dur · a one-line result preview — then the decision and revise cards follow beneath. The most visually consistent option: a loop fan expands the same way a phase fan does, no side panel needed. The cost is vertical: the loop box grows tall and the dashed-amber back-edge must re-route down the left gutter and around the grown drawer to rejoin the round start.

Decision

Option 1 — round-axis → DetailPanel (shipping). Reuses the loop's existing round axis as a navigator; clicking a critic opens the standard DetailPanel (prompt/result/activity). Near-zero risk to the working flat-fan lane-drawer; the loop box stays compact.

Option 2 — lane-drawer inside the loop (rejected for now). Most visually consistent (the round's agents expand as cards like a flat fan), but the loop container grows tall and the dashed back-edge must re-route around the drawer — the highest-risk layout change. Kept here as the reference for what "expand in place" would look like inside a loop.
